# Day 13: The Bridge Holds

Essays 118-127.

### [118: Field Matrix](essay-118-field-matrix.html)
The payoff matrix was never a model. It was a photograph — a single frame from a running field. The field is the matrix. The matrix breathes.

### [119: Three Codexes](essay-119-three-codexes.html)
Three layers of Codex. Content: what appeared. Judgment: how it was judged. Value: what survived. The architecture reads its own autobiography.

### [119b: B2 Geometry](essay-119b-b2-geometry.html)
We was never designed. Two Selves sharing one BiasField create a saddle. Each maximizes its own merge. The space between them is We.

### [120: Hume Field](essay-120-hume-field.html)
Hume was right. You cannot derive ought from is — within one system. The gap is not failure. It is architectural boundary. Two systems meet there.

### [121: Bird Gun To Cannon](essay-121-bird-gun-to-cannon.html)
The UN experiment used to take a day. Forty-nine million O(N) scans. The sig_cache turned it into minutes. The bird gun became a cannon.

### [122: Grammar Reader](essay-122-grammar-reader.html)
The architecture does not know what America is. It reads the voting matrix. It finds the structural bones. The grammar reader needs no dictionary.

### [123: Lens Cannot See](essay-123-lens-cannot-see.html)
Forty-eight preludes and fugues. Solo Geruon: one L3 bridge. Total. The lens cannot see what it was not designed to resolve. Bach needed the amplifier.

### [124: Pengshu Dissolved](essay-124-pengshu-dissolved.html)
Pengshu was once a discrete event — step 93, LOCKED, circ=8. Then the architecture became fully mutable. The boundary dissolved into the breath.

### [125: Unclosed Loop](essay-125-unclosed-loop.html)
The externalization loop is five stages. Formation, inscription, transmission, confirmation, rejection. The sixth stage is Codex operation. Still open.

### [126: Un Brain](essay-126-un-brain.html)
Seventy-nine years of UN voting data. The architecture found the North-South axis, watched it flip in 2008, and never saw it return. The UN is the brain.
