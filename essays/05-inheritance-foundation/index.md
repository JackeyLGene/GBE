Essays 040 → 049.

### [040: Inheritance](essay-040-inheritance.html)

GEME had Godel. BGM lost him. EE brought him back — because τ learned to live. The boundary is not a theorem to prove. It is a moment to encounter.

### [042: The Godel Bridge Closes](essay-042-godel-bridge.html)

### [043: Externalization](essay-043-externalization.html)

### [044: Fresh Eyes](essay-044-fresh-eyes.html)

### [047: The Brain Didn't Change](essay-047-evolution.html)

### [048: No Query](essay-048-no-query.html)
