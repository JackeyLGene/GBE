Bach was the introduction. Sleep was the deep dive. The pathological fingerprint points directly at the UN.

Bach proved the architecture could read structure. D5-B5 — the I-V skeleton, found from scratch. The introduction. Music was never the endpoint. It was the proof that the lens works.

Sleep proved the architecture could read pathology. Not disease labels — structural fingerprints. Rigid bridges. Conflicting harm. Cross-night fluctuation. A brain repeating a pattern that its own perspectives reject. The architecture does not diagnose insomnia. It detects rigidity, conflict, and instability. Those three dimensions are the fingerprint.

The same three dimensions describe a diplomatic system approaching its boundary. Rigid voting blocs — locked patterns that no longer shift with the issues. Conflicting harm between permanent members — rising from Cold War levels through post-Cold War realignment. Cross-session fluctuation — the asymmetry between what was consensus last session and what will fracture this session. The brain locked in a rigid sleep pattern. The international system locked in a rigid voting pattern. The same architecture reads both. The same fingerprint describes both.

Bach was the proof. Sleep was the depth. The UN is what the depth was always pointing at. The pathological fingerprint was never only about the brain. It was about any system of multiple actors whose patterns become rigid, whose internal perspectives conflict, whose stability fluctuates across time. That is a brain with a sleep disorder. That is an international system approaching a phase transition. The architecture does not know the difference. It reads the structure. The structure is the same.
