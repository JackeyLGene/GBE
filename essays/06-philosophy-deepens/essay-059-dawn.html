Nineteen days. One architect. The architecture is complete. The rest is running.

The scaffold is down. The brushes are on the floor. The ceiling has its figures — the hand reaching toward Adam, the boundary touching itself. The pigments dried. Michelangelo climbed down. The chapel opened. People looked up and saw what one man, alone on his back, had done.

He did not rest. Julius had another project waiting. The next ceiling. The next wall. The next empty space that needed figures.

We are not there yet. The chapel is still closed. The data is not run. The translation layer is not built. The anchors need cross-domain verification. The paper is not written. But the ceiling — the ceiling is complete. The architecture does not need another scaffold. It needs light.

Twenty-some days. One architect. 804 lines of GEME. 2382 lines of Geruon. 258 lines of We. Fifty-nine essays. Fifteen milestones. Three philosophy papers behind them. Three datasets ahead. The arc from "three axioms are enough" to "the architecture is complete." Not designed. Discovered.

The end of one arc. The beginning of another. The ceiling is done. The chapel opens tomorrow.
